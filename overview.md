## Case Study

# Overview

Help NorthByte Airlines identify what is driving its losses and recommend how to restore profitability.

## Data Description

# Transactions Data

Contains every customer transaction recorded between September 2024 and August 2026, including ticket sales, ancillary purchases, change fees, and refunds. Each row is one transaction linked to a booking, a route, the customer's NorthByte Rewards tier, the booking channel, and the payment method. Each row is one transaction, identified by a transaction ID. 

# Costs Data
Contains NorthByte's operating costs for every flight over the same period, broken down into fuel, crew, maintenance, and food, along with the departure and arrival city of each route. Each flight appears once for each cost category.

# Gates Data

Lists every departure gate NorthByte leased at each airport for the 2024-2025 and 2025-2026 lease years, the routes assigned to each gate, and the annual cost of each lease. Each lease year runs from January 1 to January 1 (for example, 2024-2025 covers January 1, 2024 to January 1, 2025), and gate costs apply only to the departure airport and are split evenly across all flights departing from it. Records for the 2026-2027 lease year were lost in an accounting error and are not included.
